# Mosaic

## Placement and release

Relate the cube's observed pose to the tool through the current grasp offset;
slip or regrasping changes that relation. For recessed pockets the complete
rotated cube footprint and bottom seating matter, not only center error. Work out
the side clearance from the task's pocket opening and cube side; when only a few
millimetres remain, align the cube's yaw and position before it enters the pocket.
For flat placement use the task's center and orientation tolerances.

Plan opening and withdrawal with the placement. Dense neighbors can obstruct the
fingertips even when the cube fits: compare grasp height, approach orientation,
placement order, and release near the support surface. Account for the remaining
fall and any movement caused by jaw opening or withdrawal; choose release height
from geometry and evidence, not a fixed drop distance. Placement, release and
withdrawal are separate events. Verify the cube after clearing it.

## Order and rework

The current order and pose affect later grasp access, placement, jaw opening and
withdrawal. Later contact can disturb completed cells: reverify affected results
when evidence changes rather than treating progress as permanent. For correction,
compare regrasping, controlled single or group pushes, temporary removal and
another allocation by contact geometry, observability and total rework cost.
Pushes are ordinary finite moves, not guaranteed alignment; inspect their effect
on neighbors.

## Collaboration

Supply labels establish availability, not ownership. Compare local placement,
direct delivery, handovers and stable staging by reach, congestion, grasp
compatibility, extra handling and concurrent work. For a handover, plan receiver
access and support together with sender release and retreat. Preserve verified
work while enabling independent work with full-arm, payload and opening-finger
clearance.
